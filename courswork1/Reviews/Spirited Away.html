Spirited Away
9.5/10

千千と千尋の神隠し (Spirited Away) is a 2001 Japanese animated fantasy film written and directed by Hayao Miyazaki, produced by Studio Ghibli. It follows Chihiro, a young girl who stumbles into a mysterious spirit world after her parents are transformed into pigs. To survive and save them, she works at a bathhouse for spirits under the watchful eye of the witch Yubaba. The film weaves Shinto symbolism, environmental themes, and coming-of-age emotion into a rich, imaginative world. It became Japan’s highest-grossing film and won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature.

By Made-up-Fan123
Posted on November 13, 2025

Spirited Away is a masterpiece of visual poetry and emotional depth. Miyazaki’s world feels both dreamlike and deeply human, filled with beauty, greed, and redemption. Every frame is alive with meaning—the soot sprites, the No-Face (无脸男), the quiet courage of a child learning to grow. Beyond fantasy, it’s a story about identity and resilience. Two decades later, it remains timeless—an animation that transcends language and culture, whispering truths about growing up, remembering kindness, and finding one’s way home.

Rating: 9/10
