Cyberpunk: Edgerunners
9.5/10

C yberpunk: Edgerunners is a 2022 Japanese animated series produced by Studio Trigger in collaboration with CD Projekt Red. Set in the neon-soaked dystopia of Night City, the series follows David Martinez, a street kid who becomes a cyber-enhanced mercenary—an “edgerunner.” The show expands the universe of Cyberpunk 2077, blending explosive action with emotional storytelling. With direction by Hiroyuki Imaishi and a synth-heavy score by Akira Yamaoka, it became a critical and fan favorite for its intensity and style.

By Made-up-Fan123
Posted on November 13, 2025

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners is a brutal and breathtaking tragedy—a high-voltage descent into love, loss, and identity. Its animation pulses with life and chaos, every frame drenched in color and sorrow. David’s rise and fall mirror the soul of Night City: dazzling, doomed, unforgettable. Studio Trigger’s visual flair meets CDPR’s moral darkness in perfect fusion. In just ten episodes, it delivers what most series never achieve—a story that burns bright, fast, and leaves scars. It’s cyberpunk at its purest: beautiful and broken.

Rating: 9/10
